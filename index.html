About Us

The Art of Elegance

Langata Nail Lounge is a modern nail sanctuary located along Langata Road, Nairobi.

We specialize in delicate, hand-crafted nail artistry — from floral motifs and pearl drops to durable acrylics and gels. Wrapped in a relaxing, stylish environment, every detail is designed to make you feel cared for from the moment you walk in.
